Max Gerlach

I am an aviation enthusiast and photographer based in Zevenbergen, the Netherlands. My interest in aircraft started at a young age, and over the past years I have been photographing at my home airport, Breda International Airport (EHSE).

My main focus is on historic aircraft — in particular the collection of the Vliegend Museum Seppe, based at EHSE. I recently became a volunteer at the museum, where I serve as Flight Coördinator.

My photos document warbirds, vintage machines and everyday operations at EHSE and beyond — at air shows, fly-ins and local events throughout the Netherlands.

I am available for commissions and events throughout the Netherlands. Take a look at my work in the gallery below!

Hope to see you at the next flight!
